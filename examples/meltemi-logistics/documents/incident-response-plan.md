## Incident management planning and preparation

This plan sets out the procedure the response team follows. Roles are
assigned before an incident, not during one.

## Assessment and decision on security events

A reported security event is triaged within one hour during office hours.
The duty officer decides whether it is an incident and categorises it as
low, medium or high.

## Response to incidents

Containment comes first, then eradication, then recovery. Every step is
recorded with a timestamp. Escalation to the Managing Director is required
for any high-severity incident.

## Learning from incidents

A post-incident review is held within ten working days. Lessons learned are
written up and the actions are tracked to closure.

## Collection of evidence

Where an incident may lead to legal action, evidence is preserved and a
chain of custody record is started.
