## Access control

Access rights are granted on the business requirement to know and on least
privilege. Nobody receives access to the transport management system
without a written request from their line manager.

## Identity management

Each person has one unique identifier. User accounts are created when HR
confirms a start date and are disabled on the leaver's last working day.

## Authentication information

Passwords must be at least twelve characters. Multi-factor authentication is
required for the customer portal and for any remote login to the office
network. Initial passwords are issued in person and must be reset at first
use.

## Access rights review

Access rights are reviewed every six months by the system owner. Accounts
that are no longer needed are revoked within five working days.

## Privileged access

Administrative access is held in separate named admin accounts, approved by
the Head of IT, and reviewed at each access review.
