## Acceptable use

Company assets are provided for work. Acceptable use of information and
equipment is limited to business purposes and reasonable personal use that
does not interfere with work or introduce risk.

## Remote working

Remote work is permitted with a company laptop and the company VPN. Public
wireless networks may be used only through the VPN. Security measures on the
device must not be disabled.

## Clear desk and clear screen

A clear desk is required at the end of each day for anything marked
Confidential. Screen lock activates after five minutes and workstations must
not be left unattended while unlocked.
