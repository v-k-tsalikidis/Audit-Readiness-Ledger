## Backup schedule

The transport management system and the customer portal are backed up every
night at 01:00. Backup retention is thirty days on site and ninety days in
the offsite copy.

## Restoration testing

A restore is tested every quarter using a sample database, and the result is
recorded in the operations log.

## ICT readiness for business continuity

The recovery time objective for the transport management system is four
hours. The recovery point objective is twenty-four hours. Disaster recovery
arrangements are exercised once a year.
