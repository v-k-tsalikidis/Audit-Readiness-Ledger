## Purpose

Meltemi Logistics moves freight for customers who trust us with their
shipment data. This information security policy states what we protect and
who is answerable for it.

## Scope

All staff, contractors and systems used to run the freight operation,
including the transport management system and the customer portal.

## Commitments

Roles and responsibilities for information security are assigned by the
Head of IT and recorded in the IT responsibilities register. Segregation of
duties is applied so that no single person can both approve and execute a
payment.

Legal, statutory, regulatory and contractual requirements that apply to us
are identified and kept in the compliance register, and reviewed once a
year.

Compliance with this policy and with the standards beneath it is monitored
by the Head of IT and reported to the Managing Director each quarter.

Independent review of information security is carried out by an external
assessor every two years.

## Related documents

Access is granted in accordance with the Access Control Policy.
Incidents are handled as per the Incident Response Plan.
Personnel matters follow the Human Resources Security Standard.
