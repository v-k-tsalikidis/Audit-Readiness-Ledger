## Perimeter

The Thessaloniki depot has a fenced security perimeter with a single
controlled vehicle gate.

## Physical entry

Entry to the office building requires an access badge. Visitors sign in at
reception, are escorted at all times, and their badge is returned on
departure.

## Monitoring

CCTV covers the depot yard and the server room door. Recordings are held for
thirty days. An intruder alarm is set outside working hours.

## Server room

Only named staff hold access to the server room. The door is monitored and
entry is logged.
