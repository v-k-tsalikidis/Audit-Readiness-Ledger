## Inventory of assets

The asset register lists every server, laptop and network device, with an
owner recorded against each one. It is maintained by the systems
administrator and updated when equipment is issued or returned.

## Classification of information

Information is classified as Public, Internal or Confidential. Shipment
manifests and customer contact data are Confidential.

## Labelling

Documents carry their classification in the footer. Labelling of removable
media follows the same scheme.

## Storage media

Removable media must be encrypted. USB devices that are not company issued
are prohibited on the office network.

## Secure disposal

Equipment is wiped before re-use and certificates of destruction are kept
for any media sent for destruction.
